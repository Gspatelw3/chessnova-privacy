
# Privacy Policy for ChessNova

Last updated: September 30, 2026

ChessNova is an offline chess game developed by GS Tech Studio.

## Information We Collect

ChessNova does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

The game does not require users to create an account or sign in.

## Internet and Online Services

ChessNova is designed to work offline. The game does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay.

## Game Modes

ChessNova provides offline chess gameplay, including:

- Local two-player mode
- Human vs Computer mode

## Advertising

ChessNova does not display third-party advertisements.

## Analytics and Tracking

ChessNova does not use analytics, tracking technologies, or advertising SDKs to monitor users.

## Data Sharing

Since ChessNova does not collect personal user data, no personal user data is sold or shared with third parties.

## Children's Privacy

ChessNova does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the features or data practices of ChessNova change. Any changes will be posted on this page.
